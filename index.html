Redex Workbench

Lean decides that two terms are equal by rewriting them until they match. Each kind of rewrite has a Greek letter, and each one replaces a term with another that means the same thing. This page lets you fire them by hand.

The rules

A redex is a part of a term that a rule can rewrite. Each row loads a small example into the workbench.

Workbench

A small colored letter marks each redex. Click it to fire that redex. Step fires the leftmost-outermost redex, To WHNF reduces only the head of the term, and Normalize runs until no redex is left. The arcs link each variable to the binder it refers to.

Every path

A term with several redexes can reduce in several orders. The graph shows all of them at once. When two paths split, they can always be joined again (the Church–Rosser theorem), so a term has at most one normal form.

Strategies

A strategy is a rule for choosing the next redex. Normal order finds the normal form whenever one exists. Applicative order evaluates arguments first, which saves work when an argument is used many times and loops when a looping argument is never used. Lean’s whnf stops as soon as the head of the term is exposed.

In Lean 4

αNothing to do
Bound variables are de Bruijn indices (Expr.bvar), so α-equivalent terms are the same Expr. Binder names are kept only for display. The workbench’s “de Bruijn” toggle shows this.
βAlways on
Every definitional equality check and dsimp uses it. Force it with beta_reduce (Mathlib). simp flag beta.
ηUsed to compare, never to compute
rfl proves (fun x => f x) = f and p = (p.1, p.2). simp flags eta and etaStruct. Mathlib: eta_reduce.
δControlled by transparency
unfold f, delta f, simp [f]. The attributes @[reducible] and @[irreducible] decide what unfolds automatically.
ζInline a let
simp flag zeta. zetaDelta also unfolds let-bound hypotheses x : α := v. intro x moves a let into the context.
ιComputation
rfl and decide run on it. simp flag iota. It gets stuck on well-founded recursion; use unfold f or f.eq_def there.
πProjections
Expr.proj reduces when its argument is a constructor application. simp flag proj. Coq counts this as part of ι.
⇥WHNF and full normal form
whnf (Mathlib tactic) reduces the head only, like the third strategy lane. #reduce t prints the full normal form.

Lean’s kernel also has rules this page does not model: Quot.lift f h (Quot.mk r a) ⟶ f a, K-like reduction of Eq.rec, GMP arithmetic on Nat literals, proof irrelevance, and η for unit-like structures.
